About Lesotho

Discover the beauty and culture of the Mountain Kingdom.

Our Country

Lesotho is a beautiful country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. It is known for its mountains, traditional culture and friendly people.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Visitors can enjoy mountain views, explore villages, learn about Basotho culture and experience outdoor adventures.
